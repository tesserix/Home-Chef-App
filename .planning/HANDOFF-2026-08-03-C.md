## #927 — why the suggested fix is wrong

The issue proposes "only bill `release_eligible`/`released`". That would **lose
chefs money**: `loadStatementOrderRows` is windowed on `delivered_at` and each
`(chef, week)` statement is generated once then frozen, so an order excluded from
its own week is never billed on any later one.

- `withheld` / `reversed` are **terminal** → safe to exclude (shipped).
- `awaiting_customer_confirmation` / `disputed` are **transient** —
  `ReleaseDisputedOrderHoldIfCleared` moves disputed → release_eligible. Excluding
  them means a dispute resolved *in the chef's favour* costs them the order.

Closing that half needs a **catch-up credit** for orders cleared after their week
closed. `ChefBonus` (the #947 mechanism) is the natural fit and already works.

## #964 — customer tips reached nobody

Checkout wrote the LEGACY `tip` column; the whole payout stack reads `chef_tip`,
which nothing had ever written. Charged to the customer, carried in
`order.total`, excluded from the chef's gross, statement, TDS base, Route split
and payout. The checkout screen promises *"100% of your tip goes to the home
chef"* — 0% did.

Fix: checkout sets `ChefTip` (100% to chef, matching the promise; `DriverTip`
stays 0 as the client sends one `tip` and offers no rider split).

**The backfill was not just a column update.** Statements are FROZEN at
generation, so setting `chef_tip` on a historical order pays nobody. So
`BackfillChefTips` does two different things:

- always set `chef_tip = tip` (earnings / PDF / TDS certificate become correct);
- raise a `ChefBonus` catch-up ONLY where a statement already covers the order.

An order not yet on a statement gets **no** credit — its next statement reads the
backfilled `chef_tip` naturally, and crediting it too would pay the tip twice.
That is the mutation-tested assertion. Runs on `payout-reconcile` (10 min),
self-limiting.

**Verified live:** repaired 3 orders, 3 credits, ₹75.00. Two further tipped
orders were correctly skipped — they are soft-deleted.

## #927 — the P0 is closed

The issue's own suggested fix ("only bill release_eligible/released") would have
LOST chefs money: `loadStatementOrderRows` is windowed on `delivered_at` and each
`(chef, week)` statement is generated once then frozen, so an order excluded from
its week is never billed by any later one. `withheld`/`reversed` are terminal and
safe to exclude; `awaiting`/`disputed` are transient — a dispute later resolved in
the chef's favour would have cost them the whole order.

So it needed a mechanism, not a predicate:

- **`orders.billed_statement_id`** — records which statement settled an order,
  written in the SAME transaction as statement creation. "Has this already paid
  the chef?" was previously only inferable, which is exactly why the two payout
  paths could not be reconciled.
- **`reconcileStatementCatchup`** (hourly) — credits via ChefBonus any order that
  became payable after its week closed.
- Only then can the predicate safely exclude transient states.

Contract: an order is paid **at most once** and **at least once** once owed.

**Verified in prod:** the backfill stamped **12** orders on the 2 existing
statements and raised **0** credits — the predicted result. Triggered manually
rather than waiting for the hourly tick (see below).

## Traps added

- **The historical-statement double-pay trap.** Pre-change statements carry no
  stamps, so their orders read as unsettled and the catch-up would have paid all
  12 again. `BackfillBilledStatementIDs` claims them first, and is scoped to
  statements with ZERO stamps — because it reproduces the OLD broad predicate,
  running it against a NEW statement would stamp orders that statement
  deliberately skipped. Both guards mutation-tested.
- **Temporal schedule IDs are prefixed.** The registered id is
  `homechef-cron-<name>`, NOT `<name>`, and the namespace is `homechef`, not
  `default`. To force a cron:
  `kubectl run tctl --restart=Never --image=temporalio/admin-tools:1.25 --command -- sh -c "temporal schedule trigger --schedule-id homechef-cron-<name> --address temporal.homechef.svc.cluster.local:7233 --namespace homechef"`
  (run detached + `kubectl logs`; `--rm -i` swallows the output).
- **Kargo is git-freight, not image-freight.** `kubectl get freight -n
  kargo-homechef -o json` has `commits`, no `images` — it tracks the Home-Chef-App
  commit. A promotion takes ~2-3 min after the build; check
  `kubectl get promotions -n kargo-homechef` before concluding a deploy is stuck.
- **GitHub Actions billing stopped CI mid-session** (4th recurrence). Repo had
  reverted to PRIVATE; jobs fail in 2-8s with a billing annotation and no logs.
  Owner authorised re-flipping to public, which fixed it — but going public also
