<objective>
GH #901 (re-scoped): allergen/diet conflict checks exist for the à-la-carte path
(`MenuItemCard`, checkout) but were never wired into the three recurring/tiffin
surfaces a subscriber sees ~20x/month — the chef-detail weekly-menu preview, the
booked meal-plan's day list, and the daily-tiffin subscribe screen. Verified against
main: the server already sends `dietaryTags`/`allergens` on `WeeklyMenuItem` (#41);
the client's `WeeklyMenuItem` type silently drops them. This is a frontend-only fix.

Purpose: make a month-long recurring commitment as safe as a single à-la-carte order —
a subscriber should never be surprised by an allergen or diet clash on a dish they
already committed to eating repeatedly.

Output:
  - `WeeklyMenuItem` (client type) carries `dietaryTags`/`allergens`; `MealPlanDay`
    carries `weeklyMenuItemId` — both already on the wire, now not discarded.
  - `WeeklyMenuDishCard` renders allergen badges + a conflict warning (matching
    `MenuItemCard`'s existing tone and styling) wherever it's shown (the chef-detail
    weekly-menu preview).
  - `MealPlanDayList` renders a per-day conflict warning on a booked plan, resolving
    each day's dish via its `weeklyMenuItemId` against the chef's weekly menu.
  - The subscribe screen (`app/meal-subscription/[chefId].tsx`) shows a single calm,
    non-blocking warning banner when the chef's weekly-menu dishes for the selected
    slots/days/variant clash with the customer's saved profile.
</objective>
